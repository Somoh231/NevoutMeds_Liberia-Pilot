# NevOut Meds: staff quick start

## Sign in
1. Open the NevOut Meds app, the same address every time. Tip: add it to your home screen.
2. Enter **your own** email and password. Never use someone else's login.
3. Check the **pharmacy name** at the top left.

## Find a product
- **Inventory → search** by name. You'll see stock, price and expiry.
- During a sale, type in **Find a product**.

## Record a sale
1. **Sales → New sale.**
2. **Customer:** search by name or phone. For someone you don't register, choose **Walk-in
   Customer**. For a new regular, use **Register them first**.
3. If the customer has **allergies** on record, a red box shows them. **Read it.**
4. **Add the products** and check the quantities.
5. **Choose the payment:** Cash, Mobile Money, … **Never choose Credit for Walk-in Customer.**
6. Press **Record sale**:
   - "**Sale recorded · Synced**" = done;
   - "**Saved on this device · Pending sync**" = you are offline; it will send by itself.

If it says **Not enough stock**, check the shelf and tell the owner. Don't record a sale for
stock you don't have.

## Add a customer
**Customers → Register customer.** First name, last name and phone are all you need. Add
allergies only if the customer tells you. **No diagnoses in notes.**

## Adjust stock
**Inventory → the product → Adjust stock.** Enter the **change**, not the new total:
- `+24` for a delivery that arrived (reason **Delivery received**);
- `−2` for damaged or expired items (reason **Damaged or expired**);
- a count correction after counting the shelf (reason **Stock count correction**).

## Create a refill reminder
**Reminders → New reminder:** choose the customer, the medicine and the refill date.

## Working offline
- Keep working. Sales, stock changes, new customers and reminders are **saved on this device**.
- The top of the screen says **You're offline**.
- When the internet comes back, everything **sends by itself**.
- Open the app while connected at the start of each day.

## The sync pill (top right)

| Says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Synced** | All saved in the cloud ✅ |
| **Offline** / **Waiting to sync** | Saved on this phone, **not yet** in the cloud. Get connected. |
| **Sync failed** | It will retry by itself. If it lasts, tap it → **Try syncing now**. |
| **Needs attention** | The server refused something. See below. |

**Don't sign out, and don't clear the browser, while it says Waiting to sync.** The app warns you
if you try to sign out.

## "Needs attention"
1. Tap the red pill and read the reason. For example: "Not enough stock to complete this sale".
2. **Tell the owner.** Don't guess.
3. The owner decides, fixes it (for example records the sale again correctly), and then presses
   **I've dealt with this — remove it**.

## Sign out
Your initials (top right) → **Sign out**. Do this when you leave a **shared** device, but only
once the pill says **Synced**.

## Help
Your initials → **Help & feedback** → **WhatsApp support**. Send a screenshot and what the
pill says. **Never send your password to anyone.**
