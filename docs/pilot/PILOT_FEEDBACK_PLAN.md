# Pilot feedback plan: first two weeks

**Purpose:** learn what works, what's missing, and whether NevOut Meds creates enough value to
continue. Each review has **one focus**, a short script, the data the operator brings, and
written outputs.

**Format:**
- Day 1 and Day 3: 15–20 minutes, by phone or WhatsApp call;
- Week 1 and Week 2: 45 minutes, in person if possible.

**Participants:** the owner, plus at least one staff member for the part about staff.

**Record everything** in the feedback log at the end of this document. Numbers come from
[PILOT_SUCCESS_METRICS.md](PILOT_SUCCESS_METRICS.md).

## Day 1 (end of the first trading day)

**Focus:** access · inventory correctness · the sale workflow · confidence offline.

Bring:
- sales recorded today, by person;
- stock adjustments;
- any "Needs attention";
- the health check.

Ask:
1. Could everyone sign in and get to work? Did anyone need help?
2. Were any sales **not** recorded in NevOut Meds today? Why? (Too slow, no customer record,
   forgot, device problem.)
3. Did any product show stock that was clearly wrong?
4. Did the internet drop? What did you see, and did you trust it would sync?
5. What was the most annoying moment today?

**Outputs:**
- a list of wrong-stock products, to recount;
- access problems, fixed;
- the top annoyance.

## Day 3

**Focus:** workflow friction · missing information · support issues.

Bring:
- sales per day (days 1–3);
- sales per staff member;
- support tickets;
- feedback submitted in the app.

Ask:
1. Which task takes longest? (Show me.)
2. What information do you look for in NevOut Meds and can't find?
3. How are walk-in sales going with the Walk-in Customer?
4. Have you sold on Credit? How are you tracking repayments?
5. Did support respond fast enough? Was anything unclear?

**Outputs:**
- the top 3 friction points, each with a severity;
- any new workaround, written into the guides.

## Week 1

**Focus:** adoption · inventory accuracy · purchasing · reports · usefulness of features.

Bring:
- active days;
- sales and adjustments per day;
- staff usage;
- the result of the 10-product recount;
- purchase orders created;
- supplier prices recorded;
- Expiry value at risk;
- feature usage (`module_view` counts).

Ask:
1. **Recount together:** 10 fast-moving products, physical vs NevOut Meds. Where does a gap come
   from?
2. Did you order through Price compare or a purchase order? Was it useful?
3. Which report or screen did you open this week, and did it change a decision?
4. Is the Dashboard's "Needs attention" list right? Is anything missing or noisy?
5. Rate each: **Sales, Inventory, Expiry, Customers, Reminders, Suppliers, Reports, Financials,
   Analyst**: 1 (useless) to 5 (essential).

**Outputs:**
- the stock-accuracy figure;
- the feature ratings;
- a priority list, draft 1.

## Week 2

**Focus:** measurable value · retention · willingness to continue or pay · priority improvements.

Bring:
- the two-week metrics against the baseline from the intake form;
- open issues;
- the priority list.

Ask:
1. What has NevOut Meds changed in how you run the pharmacy? Give one concrete example.
2. Did it help you avoid a stockout, an expired loss, or a bad price? (Check it against the
   data.)
3. Would you continue after the pilot? **If it cost US$__ per month, would you pay?** Why or
   why not?
4. If we could change **one** thing next, what should it be?
5. Would you recommend it to another pharmacy owner? (0–10)

**Outputs:**
- a continue / stop decision (owner);
- a willingness-to-pay answer;
- the agreed top 3 improvements;
- the pilot summary for the success-metrics review.
