# Adding your staff to NevOut Meds

For **pharmacy owners** (sections 1–4) and **staff** (section 3). Every person uses **their own
account and their own email address**. Please don't share one login: the app records who made
each sale and stock change.

## 1. What owners and staff can do

| | Staff | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard, Sales, Inventory, Expiry, Customers, Reminders | ✅ | ✅ |
| Record sales, adjust stock, add customers, products and reminders | ✅ | ✅ |
| Suppliers, price compare, purchase orders | ✅ | ✅ |
| Work offline | ✅ | ✅ |
| Financials, Analyst, Reports | — | ✅ |
| Staff (invite, suspend, change roles), Documents, Import data, Settings | — | ✅ |
| Delete customers, products, suppliers or orders | — | ✅ |

Staff don't see the owner screens at all, so their menu is short and simple. Most pharmacies
need **one owner**. You can make a trusted person an owner later (Staff → Make owner), but
owners can see all the money screens.

## 2. Owner: invite a staff member

1. Go to **Staff → Invite a team member**.
2. Enter their **full name** and **email address**, and choose the role **Staff**.
3. Press **Send invitation**. The app makes an invitation link for this person.
4. Press **Share on WhatsApp** (or **Copy link**) and send it **only to that person**.
5. The invitation stays open for **7 days**. If it expires, use **Resend** in the Invitations
   list.

> **While the pilot has no email sending:** a staff member who has **never had** a NevOut Meds
> account can't finish creating one from the link on their own. Tell the NevOut Meds pilot team
> their name and email. The team will verify them and send a separate **password-setup link**.
> They then open **your** invitation and choose **"I have an account"**.

## 3. Staff: accept your invitation

1. Open the invitation link from your owner **on the phone or computer you will use at work**.
2. You'll see the pharmacy's name. **Check it is your pharmacy.** If it isn't, stop and tell your
   owner.
3. Choose:
   - **I have an account**: sign in with your email and password; or
   - **Create account**: choose a password with at least 8 characters.
4. You arrive at the Dashboard. The pharmacy name is at the top left.
5. Open **Inventory** once while you're connected, so the stock list is saved on this device for
   offline use.

Your password is yours. **Nobody from NevOut Meds or the pharmacy will ever ask for it.**

## 4. Owner: check it worked

- [ ] The person appears under **Staff → Your team** as **Active**, with the right role
- [ ] They can see the pharmacy name and the product list on their device
- [ ] They **cannot** see Financials, Reports, Staff or Settings (staff role)
- [ ] They made one practice sale during training ([STAFF_QUICK_START.md](STAFF_QUICK_START.md))

## 5. When someone leaves, or loses their phone

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **A phone is lost or stolen** | Staff → the person → **Suspend**. They are signed out everywhere at once, including the lost phone. |
| **The person comes back or finds the phone** | Staff → **Reactivate** |
| **Someone leaves the pharmacy** | Staff → **Offboard**. Their past sales stay in the records with their name. |
| **Wrong role** | Staff → **Make staff / Make owner** |
| **Someone forgot their password** | Resetting by email isn't available during the pilot yet. Contact NevOut Meds support. Don't share your own login with them meanwhile. |

Every change is recorded in **Staff → Team activity log** (who changed what, and when). Nobody
can edit that log.
