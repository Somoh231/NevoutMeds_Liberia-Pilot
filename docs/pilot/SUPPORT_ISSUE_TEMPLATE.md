# Support issue template

Pharmacies can copy this into WhatsApp, or support staff fill it in during a call. The in-app
**Help & feedback → Report issue** captures the pharmacy, the user and the page automatically.

> ⚠️ **Never send your password**, not even to NevOut Meds support. We will never ask for it.

```text
NEVOUT MEDS SUPPORT
Pharmacy:
Your name:
Date and time it happened (Liberia time):
Device (e.g. Tecno Spark 10 / Chrome, laptop / Edge):
Internet at the time:   ☐ online   ☐ offline   ☐ not sure
Sync pill says:         ☐ Synced ☐ Offline ☐ Waiting to sync (how many?) ☐ Sync failed ☐ Needs attention
What were you trying to do?
What happened instead? (exact message if there was one)
What did you expect to happen?
Does this stop you working?   ☐ Yes, we can't sell   ☐ Partly   ☐ No
Screenshot attached?   ☐ Yes   ☐ No
```
